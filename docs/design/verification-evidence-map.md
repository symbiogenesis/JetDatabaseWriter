# Verification evidence map

Every critical-behavior ID in the [scope inventory](verification-scope.md) maps below to its specification, proof, executing implementation, contracts, supporting evidence and remaining gap. This is an inventory of the present boundary. It does not discharge the outstanding implementation, functional or platform requirements.

[Formal verification](formal-verification.md), [proof documentation](../../verification/README.md) and the exact [proof/dependency manifest](../../verification/config/proofs.json) define the admitted models and extraction boundary. Links to tests identify inspectable repository evidence, not an executed check for a particular revision. Execution requires a separate exact-SHA receipt and verdict. Guarded DAO skips establish no native compatibility. The [validation matrix](writer-disk-format-validation-matrix.md) and [compact evidence](compact-and-repair-evidence.md) retain the producing-engine, fixture and operation scope of existing native observations. Synthetic round trips cannot expand that scope.

## Evidence and contract labels

**Executable** means production invokes generated Dafny decisions for the named operation; the containing C# method is not thereby verified. **Abstract** means the theorem holds for its model transition relation. **Manual/tested** means source review and finite conformance or independent regression controls support correspondence without a machine-checked C# refinement. **Unspecified** means no admitted machine-checked specification exists for that behavior; design, safety and compatibility requirements still apply. **Qualification** requires independent native-engine or actual runtime/OS/filesystem/device evidence. No entry has a complete power-loss qualification receipt.

| Contract | Explicit dependency, not a proved conclusion |
|---|---|
| A1 acquisition/identity | Reported reads return their exact bytes; framing, checksum/hash and path/generation construction bind admitted records and images. Generated ranges/cursors do not prove handwritten map construction or cryptographic identity. |
| A2 effects/persistence | Successful effects have their named bytes/length result; modeled successful flushes fence exact bytes and length. Failed/partial effects grant no successful transition. Namespace discovery, directory ordering, stale-I/O quiescence and device persistence need [storage qualification](recovery-storage-contract.md). |
| A3 authority/coordination | Canonical identity, fresh object/generation/ticket identities, validated receipts and exclusive cooperating acquisition remain held through cleanup. Actual composite .NET/OS exclusion and deadlock prevention require refinement; valid record bytes alone grant no lease. |
| A4 native representation | Page/tag/token/row/field/key/collation meanings match supported native forms. Microsoft fixtures, independent byte inspection and successful named DAO operations are separate evidence. |
| A5 resources/ownership | Borrowed bytes stay stable until release; locator/pool/destination ownership and admitted aggregate work/storage bounds hold. CLR overhead, shared pools and caller resources need separate scope. Logical snapshot/allocation effects do not prove C# bookkeeping. |
| A6 compilation/runtime | Reviewed Dafny/Z3, projection including the three exact-signature controller inlining annotations, C# compiler and .NET JIT/AOT preserve generated semantics. The [trust policy](proof-trust-review.md) binds reviewed content; hashes attest freshness, not reviewer judgment, tool soundness or specification adequacy. |
