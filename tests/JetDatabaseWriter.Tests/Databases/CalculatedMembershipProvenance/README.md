The 65 `V` fields distinguish literal metadata from the stored Variant subtype.
Two literal IN operands retain numeric/Text status 13. A field read, ordinary
function, arithmetic operation, Choose or unary plus clears this discriminator
and permits formatted comparison. IIf preserves the selected branch's literal
discriminator without evaluating its inactive branch. Reading a stored
calculated field clears the discriminator even when its defining expression
was a literal or IIf.

A directly signed numeric literal retains its discriminator. Parentheses around
its operand turn it into ordinary negation: `-1 In ("-1")` caches 13, while
`-(1) In ("-1")` is true. Whitespace after the minus also clears the discriminator.
Floating negative zero retains its native text sign.
Intrinsic Boolean results use `-1`/`0` for IN comparison, including selected
IIf branches; concatenation retains its separate Boolean text rules.
