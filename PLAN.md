# Project plan

PLAN.md is the single source of truth for open project work, including formal verification. Requirement IDs remain stable; their numbers identify work rather than execution order. Design documents and verification documentation retain durable contracts, evidence and implementation details.

Open work toward reading, preserving and writing every real Microsoft JET and ACE database format and resisting malicious databases. A file's provenance or age is not a reason to reject it. Compatibility means Microsoft-produced files and documented engine behavior; this library has never shipped, so its old APIs, invented encryption formats and damaged output create no compatibility obligation. Remove obsolete implementations instead of adding shims or repair APIs for them.

Keep only open work here. A fixing commit removes the completed item (or narrows it to the remaining gap), updates affected design notes, and supplies regression evidence. Do not delete a compatibility requirement merely because safe rejection has been implemented. Distinguish Microsoft-authored fixtures, independent implementation agreement, library round trips, and unverified hypotheses. New follow-ups state their cause, reproduction or evidence gap, plan ID, dependencies and acceptance conditions. Completion requires the item-specific evidence and applicable gates in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md); publication still requires explicit authorization under [PUBLISH.md](PUBLISH.md).

- [ ] **E4 — Complete operating-system durability and coordination evidence.** Establish a fully managed directory-durability or recovery protocol on Windows/Linux/macOS (F6); flushing replacement contents alone does not persist the renamed directory entry. ## Execution and dependencies

All items belong to a workstream; independent items may run immediately without waiting for unrelated performance refactors. Dependencies follow data ownership and correctness. Every agent, including reviewers, gets a dedicated worktree. Only the integrator changes shared instructions, this plan, shared docs and final CI configuration. Agents report exact completed requirements and remaining evidence gaps; the integrator reviews and lands their work on main.

## V — Formal verification and stronger guarantees

**Compose verified production behavior and close inventoried coverage.** Maintain the critical-behavior inventory while completing serialized journal acquisition/preparation composition, replacement syscall/copy/authority refinement, complete verified recovery decision flows, physical durability qualification and parser traversal, B-tree edits and cross-process lifecycle proofs. Close the in-scope composition obligations, review specification adequacy and keep effect adapters narrow with documented residual trust. Manual correspondence and tested traces are intermediate evidence. Runtime requirements and shipping dependencies stay unchanged; abstract-model verification does not establish handwritten C# or filesystem correctness. Existing E4/F6/I15 durability and native compatibility requirements remain independent acceptance criteria.

This workstream strengthens the guarantees of the existing managed JET/ACE implementation. It adds development and CI tools first; the shipped targets, public runtime requirements and package dependency graph stay unchanged. A proof of an abstract protocol does not verify the existing C# implementation or a filesystem. Keep those evidence levels separate in documentation.

Expand the production Dafny boundary incrementally under the [boundary expansion policy](docs/design/formal-verification.md#boundary-expansion-policy). Prioritize safety-critical behavior with useful contracts and actual production invocation; source volume is not the goal. Keep storage effects and unsupported native semantics explicit while progressively removing handwritten decision logic.

The target is a composed proof foundation for an explicit inventory of critical production behaviors, supported native forms and fault models. Completion requires production to execute verified logic, or a machine-checked refinement of it, throughout that scope, with justified implementation and environmental contracts. Completing selected kernels alone does not reach this endpoint. This workstream does not certify maximum possible verification or a globally optimal architecture; V12 establishes the scope and reviews remaining opportunities under the repository's constraints.

Retain contracts, assumptions, proof coverage and implementation correspondence in [the formal verification design](docs/design/formal-verification.md) and [proof documentation](verification/README.md). Formal work complements E1/E2/E4, F6, S1 and I2/I5/I15/I17 in the other workstreams of this plan.

### Acceptance policy

- Every theorem names its fault model and assumptions. A conditional theorem must not become an unconditional README claim.
- Preserve pure managed execution on netstandard2.1 and net10.0, unchanged runtime requirements and shipping dependencies throughout the plan. Verifiers and solvers remain development tools.
- Model process failure separately from power loss, media corruption, adversarial namespace edits and caller-stream behavior. Assume neither atomic page writes nor portable directory durability without evidence.
- Safety includes retaining evidence and refusing an unsafe operation. Recovery availability is a separate obligation: under valid evidence, exclusive access and eventually successful I/O, repeated recovery must converge to the authorized image.
- Model checks are bounded evidence; inductive proofs quantify over unbounded model instances. Neither substitutes for implementation correspondence or native interoperability.
- Use generated verified logic or machine-checked refinement for in-scope safety-critical decisions and control flow. Manual correspondence arguments and conformance tests are intermediate evidence; keep their weaker status explicit until the corresponding proof obligation is discharged.
- Compose contracts across acquisition, parsing, authorization, mutation, recovery and cleanup. Each caller must establish its callee's preconditions, including identity, ownership, resource and failure-state obligations; individually proved components do not establish the complete path.
- Keep handwritten effect adapters narrow and reviewable. They perform I/O, locking and other explicitly contracted operations and report actual outcomes to verified control logic. Remove duplicated handwritten policy as verified logic takes over; moving a critical decision into a trusted adapter does not discharge its obligation.
- Review specification adequacy with reachable success and refusal cases, satisfiable preconditions and independent native evidence. An always-refusing implementation or an unreachable success theorem does not meet recovery availability or supported-operation requirements.
- Local verification, Release builds and focused tests are allowed. Full test suites and every benchmark remain hosted-only. Changes to build/test behavior require the ordinary settled-main CI gate and any new proof gate.

### V3 — Refine journal persistence and page correspondence

Cause: the buffered journal protocol constructs aligned multi-page captures and write/truncate preparation with complete original-tail coverage and zero padding beyond the file end. Record-derived minimum/replay controls and content admission compose logical mutations and buffered effects under an explicit zero-exposure contract. Unrestricted pre-marker reordering can retain exact undo evidence while exposing an unrecorded zero gap that the parser must refuse. Serialized acquisition, the zero-exposure storage/protocol boundary and production-loop correspondence still require refinement.

- [ ] Bind the constructed page payloads and capture/preparation schedules to serialized record tags, manifest/page hashes, framing and production record iteration.
- [ ] Compose verified acquisition and preparation with content admission, undo coverage and replay. Prove that the records actually admitted establish the byte, length and payload premises used by mutation and recovery; retain cryptographic primitive and storage effects as explicit contracts.
- [ ] Establish production/storage correspondence for the buffered content refinement's zero-exposure contract, or extend the protocol to safely admit pre-marker survivors where an old write or length effect reextends a truncated image. Repro: truncation persists before an older write and the resulting zero gap has no durable gap intent or replay marker; exact undo coverage alone cannot authorize parser admission (V3/V7).

Acceptance: compose the checked buffered protocol with executable page preparation and parser admission under explicit cryptographic and storage contracts. Carry its safety and progress obligations into V6's production controller; abstract progress alone does not establish C# loop refinement.
